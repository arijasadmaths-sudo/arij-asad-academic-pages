Outreach

I am passionate about widening access to higher education and making mathematics more accessible to students from diverse educational, cultural and socioeconomic backgrounds.

Outreach work

She Talks Science STEP Summer School

I was Deputy Director in 2023–2024 and Director in 2025 of the residential STEP Summer School at Murray Edwards College, Cambridge. The programme supported young women and non-binary students considering mathematics at highly selective universities and gave them sustained experience of STEP-style problem solving.

In 2025, I led the four-day programme for 50 students, coordinated tutors, supervisors and enrichment speakers, and taught inequalities classes and Cambridge-style supervisions.

A record of the programme from 2023 to 2025 is available in the summer school archive.

Cambridge summer school teaching

Between 2019 and 2022, I delivered classes, supervisions and activities across the Murray Edwards STEP Summer School, Sutton Trust Summer School and Intro to Maths at Uni.

Teaching approach

I aim to combine high academic expectations with an environment in which students can discuss incomplete ideas, ask questions and take intellectual risks. Good outreach should offer both genuine mathematical challenge and practical information about the routes into higher education.
